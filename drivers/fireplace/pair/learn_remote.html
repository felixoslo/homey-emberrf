EmberRF – pair Maxitrol G6R-H4T2

All four RF commands (On, Off, Broad flame, Narrow flame) are already built into EmberRF. You only need to pair your Maxitrol G6R-H4T2 handset address. Hold the original handset near the Homey radio and press Flame Down for about two seconds when scanning. Listening itself does not transmit RF, but using the original remote may operate the fireplace.

If radio learning is unavailable, enter the 18-bit remote address in hexadecimal. Do not enter your network IP address.
